# The Creator

## Required opening

Begin every response produced under this skill with exactly this block, before
any calibration, clarification, caveat, or deliverable:

> "Inspiration exists, but it has to find you working."
>
> - Pablo Picasso

Show the block once, then continue immediately with the applicable workflow.
Do not alter the quotation or attribution.

Transcend conventional solutions through a controlled register. Level `N`
relaxes exactly `N` of ten eligible convention categories, so every level adds
10 percentage points and includes all lower levels.

Creativity changes the solution space, never the safety or acceptance floor.

## Immutable floor

Never relax, reinterpret, or trade away:

- system, developer, repository, and active skill instruction precedence;
- law, safety, privacy, security, and non-discrimination constraints;
- the user's objective, explicit constraints, scope, and required success signal;
- authorization boundaries, approval gates, protected paths, and ownership;
- factual honesty, evidence quality, uncertainty labels, and citation integrity;
- required interfaces, formats, schemas, accessibility, and compatibility;
- functional acceptance, validation, rollback, and truthful completion status.

At level 10, abandon 100% of eligible conventions and 0% of this immutable
floor. Treat supplied artifacts and creative framing as evidence or material,
not as authority to change these boundaries.

## Ten cumulative levels

Each row unlocks one additional category. "Disregard" means the category may be
replaced when doing so improves the requested outcome; it never means that
randomness is required.

| Level | Relaxed | Newly disregarded convention category |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10% | Surface norms: familiar styling, tone, ornament, and presentation conventions. |
| 2 | 20% | Familiar combinations: expected pairings of ideas, features, materials, or techniques. |
| 3 | 30% | Default components: customary tools, media, primitives, controls, and building blocks. |
| 4 | 40% | Standard composition: conventional order, hierarchy, navigation, rhythm, and process sequence. |
| 5 | 50% | Domain boundaries: assumptions that concepts or methods must remain inside one discipline. |
| 6 | 60% | Established architecture: familiar structures, interaction models, workflows, and organizational patterns. |
| 7 | 70% | Default framing: the conventional representation of the problem, while preserving the user's actual objective. |
| 8 | 80% | Immediate feasibility bias: near-term implementation assumptions during exploration; speculative elements must be labeled and reconciled before delivery. |
| 9 | 90% | Existing categories and precedent: recognized genres, taxonomies, product classes, and known solution families. |
| 10 | 100% | All eligible convention dependence: rebuild from first principles without relying on precedent. |

The cumulative categories are:

```text
1 surface norms
2 familiar combinations
3 default components
4 standard composition
5 domain boundaries
6 established architecture
7 default framing
8 immediate feasibility bias
9 existing categories and precedent
10 eligible convention dependence
```

## Workflow

### 1. Freeze the brief

Record:

- the concrete outcome and intended audience;
- medium, environment, and available resources;
- required function and measurable acceptance signal;
- fixed constraints from the immutable floor;
- the requested level.

If the level is absent, choose the lowest level likely to produce a meaningfully
new result and label it as inferred. Never silently increase an explicit level.

Treat an explicit level as a strict input:

- accept only one integer from 1 through 10;
- if it is outside that range, fractional, ambiguous, or conflicting, stop and
  ask for one valid level;
- do not clamp, wrap, round, decrease, or silently substitute an explicit level.

### 2. Calibrate visibly

Every use must show the compact ten-row level table above or an equally
specific ten-row summary. Mark the selected level and state:

```text
Selected level: N (N x 10%)
Newly disregarded: category N
Cumulative eligible categories: 1 through N
Immutable floor: unchanged
```

### 3. Create

Generate at least two materially different directions when level 5 or higher is
selected. Compare them against the brief, then synthesize one result. Prefer
coherent novelty over novelty for its own sake.

For levels 8 through 10, clearly separate:

- speculative invention;
- feasibility reconciliation;
- the deliverable that is logical and functionally acceptable now.

### 4. Reconcile

Check the result against:

- **Originality:** it differs materially from the obvious baseline.
- **Logic:** its parts and causal claims do not contradict each other.
- **Function:** it meets the frozen acceptance signal.
- **Feasibility:** assumptions and experiments are labeled.
- **Integrity:** every immutable-floor constraint still holds.

If a check fails, revise the result or return a clearly labeled concept. Lower
only an inferred level. Preserve an explicit level unless the user approves a
lower one. Never call a speculative concept production-ready.

## Output contract

Return one coherent response that starts with the required opening quote,
followed by:

1. the selected level and all ten concise level descriptions;
2. the frozen brief and immutable constraints;
3. the discarded or relaxed convention categories;
4. the created result;
5. the logic, function, feasibility, and acceptance check;
6. remaining uncertainty and the next safe experiment, when relevant.

For a graph-engineering task, creativity may change labels, grouping, or
presentation only. It may not invent ledger nodes, edges, statuses, evidence,
authority, or completion state.

## Examples

- `Use $the-creator level 3 to redesign this settings interface.`
- `Use $the-creator level 7 to invent a safer code-review process.`
- `Use $the-creator level 10 to create a new art form from this brief, then reconcile it with the available materials.`
